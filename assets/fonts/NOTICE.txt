All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1,
the full text of which is in OFL.txt in this directory. Self-hosting and
redistribution are expressly permitted by that licence; shipping the licence
alongside the fonts is what it requires in return.

  Inter             Rasmus Andersson       https://rsms.me/inter/
  Playfair Display  Claus Eggers Sorensen  https://github.com/clauseggers/Playfair-Display
  JetBrains Mono    JetBrains              https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/

Only the latin subset of each face is shipped, and only the weights the site
actually applies. They were taken from the Google Fonts CDN on 2026-08-26 and
the CDN references were removed from the site in the same commit -- see
/corrections. The filenames carry a content hash because netlify.toml serves
them with max-age=31536000, immutable.
