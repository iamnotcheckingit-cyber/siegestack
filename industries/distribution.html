For Distributors

One industry page, because this is where the experience actually is.

This is the only industry page on this site. There is no manufacturing page, no e-commerce page and no retail page, because a page that cannot be filled with first-hand specifics is worth less than no page at all.

What distributors actually arrive with

  • A report that is fast on Today and times out on Month. Usually an access path problem that volume made visible. Performance tuning.
  • Numbers nobody trusts. Open and posted records living in separate tables, so any window longer than a day under-reports. Undated lines counted as on-time successes. The P21 data-model traps.
  • Two systems of record that were supposed to be consolidated years ago, making every network-level answer wrong in a way nobody can see. Operations modernization.
  • A nightly file holding two systems together. ERP integration.
  • A process that runs because one person remembers the order of the steps. ETL and pipelines.

Why distribution specifically

The work is on distribution ERPs and the operations around them: receiving, picking, purchasing, transfers between sites, and the reporting all of it feeds. That means the data-model traps are familiar rather than discovered on your time, and the questions asked in the first conversation are the ones that turn out to matter.

Deep hands-on experience is Epicor Prophet 21. Kinetic shares the transferable parts, and that page says so explicitly rather than implying equal depth in both.
